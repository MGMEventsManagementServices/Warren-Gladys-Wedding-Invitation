Our Story

Where Two Hearts Became One

A story of patience, timing, second chances, and a love that found its way back after many years — scroll to read the full journey of Warren and Gladys.

The Beginning

Our story started long before we knew what love really meant.

We have known each other since elementary school. We were schoolmates in Grades 1 and 2, became classmates in Grade 3, and eventually became part of the same group of friends.

Back then, we were simply kids enjoying our school days and growing up together.

Gladys remembers Warren as a bright and handsome boy. He was someone many girls had a crush on, and eventually, she found herself having a little crush on him too. But being the quiet and shy girl that she was, she kept those feelings to herself.

Warren remembers Gladys as a pretty, shy, and gentle girl. She was quiet and soft-hearted, and little by little, he started noticing her differently too.

Around Grade 5, Warren started showing more interest in Gladys.

At that age, everything was still innocent and playful. We were young and didn’t really understand what being in love meant.

But by Grade 6, our feelings started becoming more real.

We were partners in school pageants as representatives of our school, which gave us more moments together.

Then came one little moment that we still remember today.

We were assigned to clean the principal’s office when Warren gave Gladys a small letter.

It said:

“Can you be my girl?”
Yes [ ] or No [ ]

Gladys answered:

Yes.

She was with her friends Marielle and Lorener when she answered the letter. She doesn’t even know if they still remember it today.

And because we were only in Grade 6, remembering the exact date wasn’t something we thought about.

It was puppy love.

Simple, innocent, and young.

We didn’t know where it would lead.

We definitely didn’t know that years later, that little “Yes” would mean so much more.

Growing Up Together

As we grew older, so did our relationship.

Our childhood story went through its own changes, and in 2014, during our fourth year of high school, we found ourselves choosing each other again.

That became the year from which we count our relationship today.

Something also changed in the way we saw our future.

For Warren, there was already this quiet feeling inside him that someday, he would marry Gladys.

There wasn’t one big reason behind it.

He simply felt it.

For Gladys, Warren became part of her silent prayers.

She would ask God that if Warren was really the man meant for her future…

“Tani siya na lang gid.”

From childhood crushes and puppy love, our relationship slowly became something more serious.

And as we continued growing together, we learned that love becomes more meaningful when you see someone at their best—and still choose them when life becomes difficult.

Falling Deeper in Love

One of the things that shaped our relationship was learning to accept and love each other through different seasons of life.

During Warren’s teenage years, he developed skin problems that greatly affected his appearance and confidence.

It was a difficult time for him.

He struggled with his confidence and started doubting himself.

But Gladys stayed.

Warren knew that Gladys was a pretty girl and a campus crush. In his mind, she had many choices and many reasons to walk away.

But she didn’t.

She stayed beside him.

And through that experience, Warren understood that Gladys loved him for more than his appearance.

She loved him for who he truly was.

Gladys also saw love through Warren’s actions.

One thing she has always appreciated about him is his patience.

During college, Warren studied at VMA while Gladys studied at CSAB. Our school schedules were different, but Warren would still find a way to wait for Gladys.

Sometimes, he waited for more than an hour just so we could spend some time together and so he could take Gladys back to the boarding house where she stayed with her sister.

The longest wait was six hours.

And Gladys remembers thinking:

This man is unbelievable.

His patience and understanding became some of the things she appreciated most about him.

Some of our most unforgettable memories also came from the years when we didn’t have much.

We didn’t start our relationship with everything already figured out.

We experienced the simple years together.

We grew together.

We struggled together.

And those experiences helped shape the relationship we have today.

Love Across the Distance

One of the biggest changes in our relationship came when Warren started his career as a seafarer.

Before that, especially during college, we were used to being together.

Then suddenly, we had to learn how to be a couple while living thousands of miles apart.

Sometimes Warren was literally on the other side of the world.

We had different time zones.

His morning could be her night.

Her morning could be his night.

It was a difficult adjustment.

But we knew why we were doing it.

It was part of the sacrifice we had to make for the future we wanted.

So we learned.

We adjusted our schedules.

We found time to communicate.

We became more patient.

We learned to trust each other even more.

And as the years passed, we slowly learned how to live with the distance.

Being far away from each other tested our patience, trust, love, and commitment.

But it also made us stronger.

Our commitment became stronger than the distance between us.

Our prayers helped us through the difficult days.

And through all those years, God became our compass, guiding us toward the life we were trying to build together.

Knowing This Was for Life

The thought of marriage didn’t suddenly appear when the proposal happened.

It had been in our hearts for years.

For Warren, that feeling started when we got back together in 2014.

He couldn’t explain exactly why.

He just knew that someday, he wanted to marry Gladys.

For Gladys, that same future had been part of her prayers for years.

She prayed that if Warren was truly the man meant for her, she hoped he would be the person she would eventually build her life with.

We didn’t know exactly when that day would come.

We didn’t want to rush it.

We simply continued living, growing, working, and building our future together.

Until eventually, the right moment came.

The Proposal

A few months before the proposal, Warren had already bought the ring.

His original plan was to propose somewhere beautiful outside the city.

He wanted a special place and was simply waiting for the perfect timing.

Then one early morning, while Warren was heading to Iloilo for his medical examination, something changed.

While drinking his coffee, he thought about the fact that he would soon have to leave for work again.

Once again, we would have to be apart.

Gladys’s birthday was only a few days away, and she had already said that she would probably be very busy with work.

Then Warren suddenly had an idea:

Why not propose on her birthday?

That morning, he called Gladys’s older sister, Manang Donna, and told her about his plan.

She agreed.

After finishing his medical examination in Iloilo, Warren returned to Negros and asked Gladys’s mother for permission to marry her.

She gave him her blessing.

The following day, he also received permission from Gladys’s father.

Everything was ready.

Gladys had no idea.

December 9, 2025

Gladys spent almost her entire birthday working.

She wasn’t expecting anything special because she already knew how busy her day would be.

By the time she left the office at around 7:00 p.m., she was tired and a little sad.

Her birthday was almost over, and she felt like the entire day had been spent at work.

Warren arrived with flowers and told her they would have dinner together.

That simple gesture already made the day feel a little better.

We went out for dinner, enjoyed steak and some cocktails, and spent the rest of the evening together.

After dinner, Warren brought Gladys back to her apartment.

There was no grand setup waiting.

No decorations.

Nothing fancy.

Just the two of us.

Warren secretly placed his phone where it could record the moment.

His knees were shaking.

His heart was racing.

Then he hugged Gladys and asked:

“Are you happy?”

Gladys said yes.

She was happy that even though it was already late, Warren had still made her birthday special with flowers and dinner.

Then Warren asked:

“Would you be even happier if…”

He slowly went down on one knee.

He took out the ring.

And he asked Gladys to become his wife and his partner for the rest of his life.

For Gladys, everything suddenly felt unreal.

There were so many emotions at once.

She cried.

She laughed.

Then she cried again.

It wasn’t the proposal she had imagined.

There were no decorations.

There was no fancy setup.

But it was real.

It was raw.

It was just us.

And somehow, that made it even more special.

A birthday that started with work and exhaustion ended with a question that would change our lives.

And once again, Gladys answered:

Yes.

Our Journey to Marriage

Our journey has taught us that love isn’t only about the happy moments.

Love also means patience.

Understanding.

Trust.

Communication.

Commitment.

And choosing each other through every season.

We have also learned that life doesn’t need to be rushed.

Everything has its own purpose and timing.

Over the years, we allowed ourselves to grow.

We studied.

We built our careers.

We worked for our future.

We built a house that we can now call our home.

And today, we feel ready for the next chapter.

We both have stable careers.

We still have many dreams we want to achieve for ourselves and for our families.

But now, we want to continue reaching those dreams as husband and wife.

Our wedding isn’t only about the two of us.

It is also about bringing our families together.

It is a moment we have waited for and prayed for.

And now that it’s finally happening, we simply want to enjoy every part of it.

From the preparations…

to finally saying:

“I do.”

Looking Forward

We dream of building a happy and loving family.

A family filled with understanding, support, kindness, and love.

Most importantly, we want to build a God-centered family.

We hope to have our own children someday.

We want to continue building our careers and our business.

We want to enjoy the home we’ve built.

We want to reach our dreams.

And we want to continue giving back to the parents and families who have supported us throughout our lives.

There is still so much ahead of us.

We don’t know exactly what every chapter will look like.

And that’s okay.

We don’t have to rush.

One step at a time.

We want to enjoy where we are today while continuing to work for the future we dream about.

And wherever life takes us, we want to remember that we are doing this together.

Our Love Story

When we look back, our story feels almost unbelievable.

We met when we were children.

We grew up together.

A simple childhood friendship became a little crush.

That little crush became puppy love.

And one day, a Grade 6 boy handed a shy girl a letter asking:

“Can you be my girl?”

She answered:

Yes.

We continued growing.

Life changed.

We changed.

But somehow, we continued finding our way toward the same future.

We experienced the years when we didn’t have much.

We saw each other through difficult moments.

We waited for each other.

We spent years loving each other even when thousands of miles separated us.

We prayed for each other.

We built our careers.

We built our home.

And through every season, we continued choosing each other.

Then, years after that little Grade 6 letter, another question came.

This time, there were no boxes to check.

There was a ring.

And Warren asked:

“Will you be my wife?”

And just like all those years ago… the answer was Yes.

Our story has taught us that the most important things in life don’t have to be rushed.

We trust the timing.

We trust the journey.

And above all, we trust God.

Because one verse describes our story better than anything else:

“When the time is right, I, the Lord, will make it happen.” — Isaiah 60:22

And after all these years of growing, waiting, praying, and loving…

our time has finally come.
